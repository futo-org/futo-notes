### 4. Does adding an IAP change the steering picture?

- **US:**
  - The link stays allowed.
  - Adding an IAP gives Apple the prominence-parity lever from the Ninth Circuit (section 1).
  - It also settles the off-the-record report that Apple wants a US link "in addition to — but not
    as a replacement for" IAP: we would now have both.
  - The pending US commission on linked purchases applies whether or not we have an IAP. The
    district court's rate is still undecided, and the Supreme Court review (No. 25-1311) is on the
    contempt finding.
- **Non-US:** no change. The IAP does not unlock any mention of the website. The EU, Japan and
  Brazil entitlements stay unused, so their prominence rules do not apply.
- **App Store product page:** the IAP will be listed automatically under "In-App Purchases". The
  description still must not mention buying on the website (3.1.1(a) "and their metadata", and
  the Japan/Brazil pages ban it explicitly). A US-only localization could, but the listing text is
  shared across storefronts by language, so don't.
- **Play: no change.** The `play` build stays free of in-app purchases, so it stays a
  consumption-only app (Help 10281818) and keeps the "name pay.futo.org, no link" surface. Nothing
  about the iOS IAP should appear in Android copy.
