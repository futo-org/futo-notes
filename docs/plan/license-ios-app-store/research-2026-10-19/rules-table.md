### Question 3 per bucket: may a key entered in the app enable the icon choice?

- **Play, every region: yes.** The `play` build sells nothing in the app, so it is a
  consumption-only app honoring access bought elsewhere (Help 10281818). Payments policy §2 lists
  "avatars" among items that must use Play Billing *when sold in the app*; we do not sell them in
  the app. Low risk.
- **iOS, every storefront including the US: not by the letter of the rules.** 3.1.1: "If you want
  to unlock features or functionality within your app ... you must use in-app purchase. Apps may
  not use their own mechanisms to unlock content or functionality, such as license keys". The only
  escape for web-bought access is 3.1.3(b): "provided those items are also available as in-app
  purchases within the app". See the risk assessment below.
