### Native touch and focus

- _(iOS)_ Return or Done in the title transfers focus to the note's current editor, so the next keystroke edits the body. → apps/ios/Sources/Notes/Editor/TitleTextField.swift `onSubmit`, apps/ios/UITests/TextFirstSelectionUITests.swift `testTitleReturnHandsTypingToTheNativeBody`
- On-text double/triple-tap selection, selection handles, the loupe and the
  platform callout are all native on both shells; the editor adds nothing to
  them and takes nothing away, except that a long-press with the keyboard down
  lifts a block ([block drag](../mobile/block-drag.md)). _(native shells)_
- _(Android text-first editor)_ A double tap on a word selects that word even where the platform's own double tap leaves only a caret (seen on Samsung One UI 8.5, Android 16); a double tap on spaces or punctuation adds no selection. → apps/android/app/src/main/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/LineSelectTouch.kt, apps/android/app/src/androidTest/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/TextFirstTapGestureTest.kt
- _(Android text-first editor)_ A triple tap selects the logical line, including its wrapped rows, and opens the platform's selection handles and callout on every sequence. → apps/android/app/src/main/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/LineSelectTouch.kt, apps/android/app/src/androidTest/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/TextFirstTapGestureTest.kt
- _(Android text-first editor)_ Platform classification supplies smart callout actions without asynchronously expanding or restoring an earlier selection range. → apps/android/app/src/main/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/SelectionTextClassifier.kt, apps/android/app/src/androidTest/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/TextFirstTapGestureTest.kt
- _(Android text-first editor)_ Select All preserves the note's current scroll position, and so does ending it: closing the text menu (Back) or dropping the keyboard ends the range at its end without moving the note. → apps/android/app/src/main/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/TextFirstEditTextReveal.kt, TextFirstRenderHold.kt, apps/android/app/src/androidTest/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/TextFirstCaretFollowTest.kt, TextFirstBlurTest.kt
- _(Android text-first editor)_ Gboard's Text editing Select mode and hardware Shift+arrow keys extend and narrow selections in note text and table cells, including the first selection immediately after typing in a new note; releasing Shift returns arrows to ordinary caret movement. Selection movement changes neither note bytes nor Undo history. Verified on the emulator 2026-11-07. → apps/android/app/src/main/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/EngineInputConnection.kt, apps/android/app/src/main/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/TableCellEditText.kt, apps/android/app/src/main/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/ChunkedSelectionNotifications.kt, apps/android/app/src/androidTest/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/TextFirstSelectionKeysTest.kt, apps/android/app/src/androidTest/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/ChunkedEditableTest.kt
- _(Android text-first editor)_ Inline formatting and background styling retain the native keyboard selection anchor when displayed text and the canonical range stay unchanged; the next Shift+arrow continues from the same end. Full resets and changed canonical ranges apply their new selection. Verified on the emulator 2026-11-07. → apps/android/app/src/main/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/TextFirstEditTextUpdates.kt, apps/android/app/src/main/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/TextFirstEditTextStyling.kt, apps/android/app/src/androidTest/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/TextFirstSelectionKeysTest.kt
- _(Android text-first editor)_ While the note is scrolled, everything drawn over the content reaches the editor's top edge as the content does, in one shade: selection tint on text, code, table cells and photos, find marks and the current match, code-block backgrounds. → apps/android/app/src/main/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/TextFirstEditTextCode.kt `drawTopPaddingBand`, apps/android/app/src/androidTest/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/TextFirstCaretFollowTest.kt, TextFirstImageTintTest.kt
- _(Android text-first editor)_ Every overlay on a row of text, a selection, a find mark, the current match, a selected list marker, a selected line break, is drawn on one band: as tall as the row's text box and centred on its letters as seen (capital tops to descender bottoms), the same for selection and find on the same row, in prose, headings and code; consecutive selected rows keep the line spacing between them. → apps/android/app/src/main/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/OverlayBand.kt `overlayBand`
- _(Android text-first editor)_ A selection, on one row or several, tints each row over its own text, from where that row's text starts (a list's or quote's hanging indent, a right-to-left row's right side), never the margin left of it; every selected line break shows a space's width of tint after its row's text (left of it on a right-to-left row), one shape with the text's tint, on a paragraph's last visual row only: a soft wrap and the note's last line show none, and an empty row shows only that space. Copy and Cut carry those breaks. A list item's marker is its first character, tinted when the selection covers it (mobile/lists.md). → apps/android/app/src/main/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/TextSelectionPaint.kt, SelectedPartOfRow.kt, `SelectedPartOfRowTest`, `TextFirstWrappedSelectionTintTest`, `TextFirstLineBreakCopyTest`
- _(Android text-first editor)_ A tap that leaves a caret shows the caret's whole line, even when the caret was already there: a retap on a line half under the note's edge scrolls it into view instead of leaving a clipped caret. → apps/android/app/src/main/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/TextFirstEditTextReveal.kt, `TextFirstRetapRevealTest`
- _(Android text-first editor)_ A caret or selection end carried to the note's top or bottom edge, by its handle or by a hold that drags a selection, scrolls the note at the block drag's ramp (faster the nearer the edge) for as long as the finger stays in the edge zone, whatever is under it (a photo taller than the zone, a code block, a table), and the end lands on the caret stop nearest the finger. On Android 10 and later the handles are found by public window structure; where they are not (an OEM's own editor, Android 9) a handle keeps the platform's own scroll. → apps/android/app/src/main/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/CaretEdgeScroll.kt, HandleWatch.kt, `TextFirstCaretEdgeScrollTest`
- _(Android text-first editor)_ An editor-driven caret or selection edge scroll has no haptics: the platform's handle tick is suppressed while the editor drives the scroll. Ordinary handle dragging uses the platform's feedback. → apps/android/app/src/main/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/TextFirstEditText.kt `performHapticFeedback`, CaretEdgeScroll.kt
- _(Android text-first editor)_ An action that does not move the caret does not move the note: a task checkbox toggled, a format applied or a block copied elsewhere leaves the reader where they are; only an edit at the caret, or a caret that moved, is revealed, with the least scroll that shows it. A note opens with its caret where the session can hold one (after front matter), so the first action does not move it there. → apps/android/app/src/main/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/TextFirstEditTextUpdates.kt, EditorEngineStorage.kt, `TextFirstCheckboxScrollTest`
- _(Android text-first editor)_ Note text scrolls up to the editor's top edge and is clipped there, never drawn over the title row above it; while the note is scrolled a 1dp border-colour hairline marks that edge, so a heading cut at it reads as scrolled under the title row. Verified emulator 2026-11-05 (pancakes and journal H1s, keyboard open). → apps/android/app/src/main/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/TextFirstEditTextCode.kt `drawScrollEdge`
- _(Android text-first editor)_ The keyboard receives canonical selection and composition coordinates even while the visible note is a prefix. Surrounding context is bounded and surrogate-safe; an oversized selected range omits keyboard context while canonical Copy and input replacement retain the full selection. → apps/android/app/src/main/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/EngineImeState.kt, apps/android/app/src/main/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/TextFirstIme.kt, apps/android/app/src/main/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/EngineInputConnectionModern.kt, apps/android/app/src/androidTest/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/TextFirstStreamingCommandsTest.kt
- _(Android text-first editor)_ A keyboard correction that selects text and commits its replacement inside one batch edit (Gboard's double-space period) is one [text-service replacement](../portable-core/interpretation-and-input.md): Undo restores the source and the collapsed caret the correction started from, not the range it selected, and the next typed text lands there, also when the keyboard reads the selected text, the text around the caret or the extracted text during the batch. → apps/android/app/src/main/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/EngineInputConnection.kt, apps/android/app/src/androidTest/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/TextFirstExitTest.kt `undoAfterKeyboardPeriodRestoresTheOpeningCaret`, `readingTheKeyboardReplacementRangeKeepsTheOpeningCaret`
- _(Android native text-first editor)_ Tapping to move the caret settles any keyboard composition or reconversion before selecting the tapped position; leaving a suggested word does not require accepting a keyboard suggestion. → apps/android/app/src/main/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/EditorEngine.kt `select`
- _(Android native text-first editor)_ A tap or handle drag made while an earlier command is still running (a whole-note format toggle) is applied after that command, carried through its display edits, and shown again over the command's result: the next typed key lands at the tapped caret, never over the selection the tap left, and a tapped table cell still opens. → apps/android/app/src/main/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/ViewEditJournal.kt, apps/android/app/src/main/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/EditorEngineTables.kt `selectDisplayRange`, apps/android/app/src/androidTest/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/TextFirstTapDuringCommandTest.kt
