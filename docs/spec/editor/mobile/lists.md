# Lists, checkboxes and links

- *(mobile)* A list item's bullet, number or checkbox is its first character, the engine's marker unit (portable-core lists.md), drawn once, in the item's marker column: one list step wide, or the whole number and its gap when wider. Wrapped and continued rows hang at the item's text. A selection tints the marker exactly when it covers that character, an empty item's included; the indent before it is never tinted. → apps/android/app/src/main/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/TfListMarker.kt, apps/android/app/src/main/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/ListMarkerColumn.kt, apps/android/app/src/main/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/TextFirstSpans.kt `TfList`, apps/android/app/src/test/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/EngineStyleSpansTest.kt, apps/android/app/src/androidTest/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/TextFirstListSelectionPaintTest.kt, apps/android/app/src/androidTest/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/TextFirstGlyphMetricsTest.kt, apps/android/app/src/androidTest/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/TextFirstListMarkerTouchTest.kt `aMarkerIsDrawnOnceInItsOwnColumn`, apps/ios/Sources/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstLayoutFragment+ListMarkers.swift, apps/ios/Sources/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstTypesetDelegate.swift, apps/ios/Sources/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstStyler.swift, apps/ios/Tests/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstListMarkerTests.swift `aMarkerUnitSpansItsColumnBetweenItsTwoCaretStops` / `listMarkersCenterOnTheirTextAndStayPutWhenTypedInto` / `aMarkerUnitIsItsColumnInAnyDirection` / `aContinuedPlainLineHangsAtItsItemsText` / `aContinuedLineHangsAtAWideNumbersText`, apps/ios/Sources/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstEditorController+ListSelection.swift, apps/ios/Tests/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstListSelectionTests.swift
- *(mobile)* An item whose first word is wider than its row (a link, a long word) keeps its marker on that word's first row, as before marker units. On Android the view holds the marker unit as a no-break space (the engine's U+FFFC, one character for one, so every offset is the engine's), sized to the marker column and drawn clear, on every API level: a U+FFFC or a replaced run would let the line break right after it; the keyboard, copy and find read the engine's text. On iOS the line breaker is refused the break after the unit. → apps/ios/Sources/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstLayoutFragments.swift `TextFirstLayoutDelegate`, apps/ios/Tests/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstListMarkerTests.swift `aLongFirstWordStaysOnItsMarkersRow`, apps/android/app/src/main/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/ListMarkerUnitCharacter.kt, TfListMarker.kt, apps/android/app/src/test/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/ListMarkerUnitCharacterTest.kt, apps/android/app/src/androidTest/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/TextFirstListMarkerWrapTest.kt
- *(mobile)* A tap on a bullet or number acts as on a character: on the leading half of its marker column (the left, the right in a right-to-left item) the caret goes just before the marker, on the other half to the start of the item's text. A tap on a marker or a checkbox beside the caret opens no Paste/Select menu. → apps/android/app/src/main/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/TextFirstEditTextTouch.kt, apps/android/app/src/androidTest/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/TextFirstListMarkerTouchTest.kt `aTapOnEitherHalfOfAMarkerPutsTheCaretOnThatSide`, apps/ios/Tests/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstListMarkerTouchTests.swift `aTapOnAMarkersHalvesPlacesTheCaretOnThatSide`, apps/ios/Sources/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstEditorController+Input.swift `editMenuForTextIn`, apps/ios/Tests/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstListMarkerTouchTests.swift `aTouchOnAMarkerOpensNoCaretMenu`, apps/ios/UITests/TextFirstListMarkerUITests.swift
- *(mobile)* With the keyboard up, a hold on a marker selects just that marker, with handles and Cut and Copy, so Copy gives its source (`10. `); with the keyboard down a hold lifts the block (block-drag.md). → apps/android/app/src/main/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/ListMarkerTouch.kt, apps/android/app/src/main/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/NotePlatformLongClick.kt, apps/android/app/src/androidTest/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/TextFirstListMarkerTouchTest.kt `aHoldOnAMarkerWithTheKeyboardUpSelectsItAndCopiesItsSource`, apps/ios/Sources/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstEditorController+MarkerHold.swift, apps/ios/Tests/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstListMarkerTouchTests.swift `aHoldSelectsJustTheMarkerAndTicksNoBox`, apps/ios/UITests/TextFirstListMarkerUITests.swift
  > **Gap:** *(mobile)* Share, Look Up, Translate and the other system text actions receive a selected list marker not as its text: on iOS as the object-replacement character U+FFFC, as they do a divider or an image; on Android as a no-break space.
- *(mobile)* A hold on a checkbox selects it, as any marker, and ticks nothing (a tap ticks it, below). → apps/android/app/src/main/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/TextFirstEditTextTouch.kt, apps/android/app/src/main/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/TextFirstCheckbox.kt, apps/android/app/src/androidTest/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/TextFirstListMarkerTouchTest.kt `aCheckboxTicksOnATapAndIsSelectedNotTickedByAHold`, apps/ios/Sources/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstEditorController+Taps.swift `checkboxFragment`, apps/ios/Sources/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstEditorController+MarkerHold.swift, apps/ios/Tests/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstListMarkerTouchTests.swift `aHoldSelectsJustTheMarkerAndTicksNoBox`, apps/ios/UITests/TextFirstListMarkerUITests.swift
- *(Android text-first editor)* Right after a marker the keyboard capitalises as at a line's start. → apps/android/app/src/main/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/ImeCapsMode.kt, apps/android/app/src/test/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/ImeCapsModeTest.kt
  > **Gap:** *(iOS)* Right after a marker (at a non-empty item's text start) the keyboard does not capitalise: it reads the marker's U+FFFC before the caret as the middle of a sentence. The keyboard asks UITextView's own tokenizer; replacing it stopped taps placing the caret, and wrapping it crashed the app.
- *(mobile)* Text typed right after a marker looks like ordinary text of its item: nothing of the marker's own drawing (its hidden character, its width) carries to it, also while it is composed or dictated. → apps/ios/Sources/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstEditorController+AtomTypingAttributes.swift, apps/ios/Tests/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstListMarkerTests.swift `typingAfterAMarkerUnitIsPlainText`
- *(mobile)* A quote's `>` is not a unit: it stays hidden and is drawn as the quote's bar, and in a quoted item only the list marker is a unit (portable-core lists.md). → apps/ios/Sources/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstStyler.swift, apps/ios/Tests/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstBlockDrawingTests.swift `aBulletCanBeAddedInsideAQuote`
- *(mobile)* A tap on a task's checkbox, on an external link's own glyphs or on a resolved wikilink acts on that control alone: the checkbox toggles, the link opens, the note opens, and the tap places no caret and raises no keyboard (the caret stays where it was). A tap on the text beside them is ordinary text interaction. → apps/android/app/src/main/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/TextFirstEditText.kt, apps/android/app/src/main/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/ExternalLinkTouch.kt `linkAt`, apps/android/app/src/androidTest/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/TextFirstLinkTapGestureTest.kt `aTapOnEachWrappedLineOfALinkOpensItAndPlacesNoCaret` / `aTapOnAnUnbrokenWrappedLinkOpensItAndPlacesNoCaret`, apps/ios/Sources/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstEditorController+Taps.swift `checkboxFragment` / `toggleTask`, apps/ios/Sources/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstEditorController+Links.swift, apps/ios/Tests/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstLinkTests.swift, apps/ios/Tests/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstListMarkerTouchTests.swift `aCheckboxIsHitOnItsMarkerUnitOnly` / `aCheckboxTapLeavesTheViewAndTheEngineOnOneSelection`
- *(Android)* A checkbox tap while text is selected (a held checkbox, a word) ends the selection and leaves the caret at its end: the tick changes the text, which takes the selection's handles and menu away, so no tinted range without handles stays behind, and a hold on a word then selects that word. → apps/android/app/src/main/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/TextFirstEditTextTouch.kt, apps/android/app/src/androidTest/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/TextFirstListMarkerTouchTest.kt `aTickEndsASelectionAndAHoldOnAWordThenSelectsTheWord`

- *(iOS)* An empty checklist row keeps its checkbox at the same baseline when its first character is typed or deleted. → apps/ios/Sources/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstLayoutFragment+ListMarkers.swift, apps/ios/Tests/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstListMarkerTests.swift `anEmptyTaskKeepsItsCheckboxPositionWhenTypedInto`
- *(iOS)* The Link prompt starts in the destination field when its label is already supplied by the selection; an unchanged label keeps inline formatting. A destination typed without a scheme that looks like a web address (it starts with `www.`, or ends in a well-known public domain such as `.com` or `.org`, and is not a `.md` note) is saved with `https://` in front, so what the note holds is what the link opens; anything the editor is unsure of is saved as typed. On tap, only a destination with an explicit scheme (`http`, `https`, `mailto`, `tel`, `sms`) opens outside the app, behind the external-open guard; `javascript:`, `file:` and `data:` never open. A destination without a scheme is a note path, resolved from the current note's folder: the note opens if it exists and otherwise nothing happens, and a `#fragment` alone does nothing. A hand-written `[x](google.com)` therefore does not open a browser. → apps/ios/Sources/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstEditorController+Links.swift, apps/ios/Tests/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstLinkTests.swift, crates/futo-notes-editor/src/link_target.rs, crates/futo-notes-editor/tests/link_target.rs
- *(Android)* After Enter in a list, its Undo and its Redo, Indent and Outdent, every item is drawn as a fresh open of the note draws it: an item beside the edit keeps its own marker, checkbox and strike, and a checked task's strike crosses its text, never its box. → apps/android/app/src/main/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/EditKeepingRowSpans.kt `replaceKeepingRowSpans`, EditorEngineWindow.kt `planStyleWindow`, TextFirstEditTextStyling.kt `setRendererSpan`, apps/android/app/src/androidTest/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/TextFirstStructuralEditRenderTest.kt, TextFirstListMarkerTouchTest.kt `aCheckedTasksStrikeNeverCrossesItsBoxWhicheverSpanIsNewer`
