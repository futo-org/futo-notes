# Block drag on iOS: the move

Lines of [block drag](../block-drag.md), whose introduction holds for both platforms.

- *(iOS)* The note shows the move live while the block is carried (the web editor's reflow): the block is dimmed to 35% in its place until the gap first moves; from then on its place closes up, a gap opens where a release would land with a copy of the block in it at 35%, and the blocks between slide to make room. No drop line is drawn with the live preview. The note itself is not changed until the drop: the preview is a curtain over the visible note, painted with the editor's own surface colour (light and dark), holding slices of the note drawn by the editor's own drawing, so it shows exactly what the screen showed when it appears. The slices are drawn in idle time after the lift. → apps/ios/Sources/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstReflow.swift, apps/ios/Sources/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstSlicePainter.swift, apps/ios/Tests/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstBlockReflowTests.swift
- *(iOS)* The gap is chosen from the finger's height only, starting from the gap open now: it moves past the stretch of note between it and the next place it may open (an engine slot, or the block's own place) once the finger passes that stretch's middle as it is drawn now (mid-slide included), and stays on the exact middle. Where the engine offers no slot (inside a quote, a code block, a table, a list that takes nothing) the finger has to pass the whole stretch up to the next slot. The block's own place means nothing moves. A stretch passed is drawn on the gap's other side, a block's height away, so a finger resting near a boundary between blocks of different heights does not make the gap flicker. iOS delivers a finger's moves once per frame, and the gap is judged once for each. → apps/ios/Sources/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstReflowPlan.swift `gap(from:finger:)`, apps/ios/Tests/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstBlockReflowTests.swift
- *(iOS)* The blocks between slide over 0.18 s with the web editor's easing (cubic-bezier 0.2, 0.9, 0.3, 1), from wherever they are drawn mid-slide; a block whose drawing first shows is drawn in its place at once. → apps/ios/Sources/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstReflow.swift `TextFirstReflowMotion`
- *(iOS)* A photo lifted out of a line of text (`Nice!![](a.jpg)`) previews like any block: its line closes up without it as the line will be laid out once the photo has left (laid out a second time, off screen, at the lift), and the photo sits in the gap as the line of its own it becomes (at the text's margin, its own height). Its place is its middle: the gap opens above or below its own line once the finger passes the middle of the part of the line above or below that. → apps/ios/Sources/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstClosedLine.swift, apps/ios/Sources/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstEditorController+BlockReflow.swift `reflowPlan`, apps/ios/Tests/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstBlockReflowTests.swift
- *(iOS)* The drag shows the drop line instead of the live preview with Reduce Motion on at the lift (nothing slides), and when the preview could not be built, logged with its reason; never because of a block's height or kind. With the drop line the block's place stays dimmed and the blocks around it do not move. → apps/ios/Sources/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstEditorController+BlockPreview.swift `startPreview`, apps/ios/UITests/TextFirstBlockReflowUITests.swift
- *(iOS)* At the lift the view asks the engine, in one call, only for the slots inside what TextKit has laid out (the screen and a margin), so a lift is as quick in a long note as in a short one. As the note scrolls it asks only for the stretch that newly comes near the screen, keeping the slots by their place in the text, never by where they were drawn at the lift. Every slot the engine offers is one where the drop will be accepted, so a drop line never promises a move that springs back. → apps/ios/Sources/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstEditorController+BlockLift.swift `liftPlan`, apps/ios/Sources/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstEditorController+BlockMove.swift `blockSlots`
- *(iOS)* With the drop line, it marks the engine's slot whose boundary is nearest the finger, among the slots that show; when one of the block's own two edges is nearer there is no drop line. A lifted photo has no such edges (every slot moves it, its own line's edges too): there is no drop line while the finger is nearer the photo's middle than any slot. A photo alone on its line lifts its line, and the card, the dimmed area and those two edges are the line's, even on the note's last line where the photo's range and its line's are the same; the view tells the two apart by whether what the engine returned runs from a line's start to a line's start. → apps/ios/Sources/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstEditorController+BlockDrag.swift `TextFirstDropTargets`, apps/ios/Tests/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstBlockDragTests.swift
- *(iOS)* With the live preview, a release with a gap open moves the block there as one Undo step. The card goes at once and the copy in the gap turns solid; the move is made at once under the curtain, then the slices and the copy slide the few points left to where the moved note draws them (a box, an indent or a number the block gained on arrival fades in), and the curtain comes down when the slides end, at most 0.4 s after the release, uncovering the same picture. Nothing that shows jumps at the drop; past its foot (left shorter, or a cancel there) it slides back. No block lifts until the curtain is down; a scroll, rotation or relayout while it settles takes it down at once. A release with the gap at the block's own place, or onto a move the engine refuses, changes nothing: the card goes and what slid slides back. → apps/ios/Sources/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstEditorController+BlockLanding.swift `landPreview`, apps/ios/Tests/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstBlockReflowTests.swift, apps/ios/UITests/TextFirstBlockReflowUITests.swift
- *(iOS)* With the drop line, releasing over it moves the block there as one Undo step. Releasing with no drop line, or onto a move the engine refuses, changes nothing and the card slides back onto the block where it is drawn now (a photo above it may have loaded since the lift). → apps/ios/Sources/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstEditorController+BlockDrop.swift `endBlockDrag`, apps/ios/UITests/TextFirstBlockDragUITests.swift
- *(iOS)* Held within 64 pt of the top or bottom edge of what shows of the note, a lifted block scrolls the note: 200 pt/s at the zone's inner edge ramping to 1400 pt/s at the edge and beyond it, every frame while the finger holds still (a block lifted already inside a zone scrolls at once, and the speed follows the visible area when it changes under a still finger, as on rotation), a frame's step at most 0.05 s of travel, stopping at the note's ends and whenever the drag ends, however it ends. Where what shows is shorter than two zones the nearer edge wins. → apps/ios/Sources/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstBlockDragTargets.swift `TextFirstAutoscroll`, apps/ios/Tests/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstBlockDragScrollTests.swift, apps/ios/UITests/TextFirstBlockDragAutoscrollUITests.swift
- *(iOS)* The live preview carries on while the note autoscrolls: text scrolling in is drawn at once where the preview puts it, as it draws now (a photo that loads is drawn again), and the scroll reaches the note's end, gap and all. A block not all laid out is laid out apart for its height; its copy keeps its own drawing. → apps/ios/Sources/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstEditorController+BlockReflow.swift `reflowPlan`, apps/ios/Tests/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstBlockReflowFarTests.swift
- *(iOS)* While the note scrolls under a still finger the gap, or the drop line, follows the slot now under it, those scrolling into view included, each read from TextKit once laid out. A change of slot because the note scrolled does not tick; only the finger's own movement does. → apps/ios/Sources/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstEditorController+BlockLift.swift `dropTargets`, apps/ios/Tests/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstBlockDragScrollTests.swift, apps/ios/Tests/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstBlockReflowTests.swift
- *(iOS)* A block carried by the autoscroll lands where the preview (or the drop line) showed, upwards or downwards, and the note does not jump at the drop: if the block left from above the screen, the lines on screen stay where they were drawn, not everything moving up by the block's height. A block lifted on screen closes up its place as before. → apps/ios/Sources/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstEditorController+BlockLanding.swift `commitUnderPreview`, apps/ios/Sources/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstEditorController+BlockDrop.swift `holdStill`, apps/ios/UITests/TextFirstBlockDragAutoscrollUITests.swift
- *(iOS)* A change of layout alone during a drag (a photo finishing loading, a rotation) measures the drop line, the dim, the slots and the live preview again from where they are drawn now, and the drag carries on: the gap stays on its slot (or goes home if that slot no longer shows) and is judged again under the still finger against the runs as drawn, as a finger move is, without a tick (a gap so opened shows at once); a slice drawn again starts from where the old one was drawn. → apps/ios/Sources/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstTextView.swift `layoutSubviews`, apps/ios/Tests/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstBlockDragScrollTests.swift
- *(iOS)* A change to the note's text during a drag (a sync, an outside write, anything that moves the engine's revision) cancels it at once, with the finger still down. A sync that writes the note ends the drag as soon as the note's screen hears of it, before the editor has taken the new text in, and no block lifts or drops until it has: the card, the live preview, the drop line and the dim go at once, nothing moves, and there is no drop haptic. A text change while a drop is settling takes its curtain down at once. What the finger does until it lifts is not a drag. → apps/ios/Sources/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstEditorController+BlockDrag.swift `cancelBlockDrag`, apps/ios/UITests/TextFirstBlockDragAutoscrollUITests.swift
- *(iOS)* A drag also ends with nothing moved when iOS cancels the touch, when the app stops being active (an incoming call, a switch to another app, going to the background), and when the note closes or leaves the screen (another note opened over it). → apps/ios/Sources/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstEditorController+BlockDrag.swift `blockDragInterrupted`, apps/ios/Tests/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstBlockDragScrollTests.swift, apps/ios/UITests/TextFirstBlockDragAutoscrollUITests.swift
- *(iOS)* A second finger on the note during a drag is ignored: it neither ends the drag nor moves the card, places no caret and scrolls nothing. → apps/ios/UITests/TextFirstBlockDragAutoscrollUITests.swift
- *(iOS)* A debug build's puppet `state` reports a drag in progress as `blockDrag`: the display line lifted, how it shows the move (`preview`: `reflow` or `dropLine`), where a release lands (`lands`: the line it would land above, or the note's end; `dropLine` the same while the drop line shows), and what is drawn in screen points (the drop line's y, null while hidden; the card's frame; the scroll offset; with the live preview, `reflow`: whether its curtain shows and the frame of the copy in the gap). `previewUnavailable` gives the logged reason when the drag has no preview it should have. `landsY` is where the slot a release lands on is laid out now (screen y), and `lastLanding` how long the last drop's curtain stayed after the release. `dragPreview` is true while a preview or a settling drop covers the note; `ticks`, `autoscroll` its ticks, last second's scroll cost; `scroll` offset and foot. A debug build launched with `-FUTODebugReduceMotion` drags as with Reduce Motion on. → apps/ios/Sources/App/PuppetNative/PuppetNativeEditor.swift
- *(iOS)* A block dropped into a list arrives as the engine writes it ([block arrival](../../portable-core/block-arrival.md)): a plain line or a photo dropped into a checklist becomes an unchecked task, a bullet dropped into a numbered list takes its number and the items below count on, a nested item dropped among top-level items comes out to their level, and an item dropped outside any list goes to the left margin. The card, the dimmed place and the copy in the gap show the block as it looked where it was lifted; the changes appear at the drop (fading in as the copy settles), in the same Undo step. → crates/futo-notes-editor/src/engine/commands/lists/arrival.rs, crates/futo-notes-editor/src/engine/tests/moves/arrival.rs
- *(iOS)* Haptics, as the web editor's: a medium impact at the lift, a selection tick each time the finger moves the drawn gap, or carries the drop line, to a slot it was not on (none while it stays, none for the block's own place, none when the layout or the scroll changes under a still finger), and a light impact only when a move is made. A release that moves nothing is silent. → apps/ios/Sources/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstEditorController+BlockDrag.swift `TextFirstDragHaptics`, `TextFirstBlockDrag.follow`, apps/ios/Tests/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstBlockDragTests.swift
