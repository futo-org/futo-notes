# Block drag on Android

Lines of [block drag](../block-drag.md), whose introduction holds for both platforms.

The Android editor (#444, reflow #450) follows the iOS lines ([lifting](ios-lift.md), [the move](ios-move.md)) where they say what a lift and a
drop do; it differs where a line below says so. Its lift and card are
apps/android/app/src/main/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/BlockLift.kt and BlockDrag.kt, its
reflow preview BlockReflow*.kt; its engine calls EditorEngineBlocks.kt.

- *(Android)* With no text field of the note's screen focused (the body, an open table cell, the title or Find), holding a finger 340 ms within 10 dp anywhere on a line of the note body lifts what the engine says a press at the position under the finger lifts: on a photo (inside the box it is drawn in, so not the blank part of its row beside a shorter photo, and to the left of its caret position in a right-to-left run), that photo's position; elsewhere the character nearest the finger on that row, whole (right of a row's last character, that character's start; a position inside a photo the finger is beside steps past it). A finger that starts scrolling the note first (past the platform's touch slop, 8 dp) and keeps moving lifts nothing; once the note's scroll or a table's sideways scroll has claimed a touch, it lifts nothing however long it then rests. Only a finger lifts: a stylus or a mouse keeps the platform's long press and selection. → apps/android/app/src/main/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/BlockLift.kt `LiftPolicy`, BlockDragGeometry.kt `pressOffset`, apps/android/app/src/androidTest/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/TextFirstBlockDragTest.kt
- *(Android)* While a field is focused, a hardware keyboard's included, no press lifts and the long-press is the platform's: word selection with its handles and text menu. → apps/android/app/src/main/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/BlockLift.kt `mayLift`, TextFirstController.kt `liftAllowed`, apps/android/app/src/androidTest/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/TextFirstBlockDragTest.kt
- *(Android)* The press asks the engine at 340 ms; the platform's long press (at the system's long-press timeout) waits for the answer and runs only where nothing lifts: a blank line, the empty last line after a final line ending, front matter, code nothing closes, an empty note. If the finger lifts before the engine has answered, the platform's long press it held for runs then. A press that lifts gets neither the platform's selection nor its text menu; taps, double- and triple-taps, a checkbox tap, scrolling and a table's sideways scroll are unchanged. A table's grid, a link and a checkbox are held like any other part of their line. → apps/android/app/src/main/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/BlockLift.kt `holdsLongPress`, TextFirstEditTextTouch.kt, apps/android/app/src/androidTest/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/TextFirstBlockDragTest.kt
- *(Android)* While the note is still opening (before its editor session exists) no press lifts. Once it is open, a lift in a note whose end is still streaming into the view lifts at once from what the view holds, as quickly as in a short note, and the rest keeps arriving during the drag in the same bounded slices (a lifted block's finger does not pause them, as a resting finger does), so the UI never stalls for it; the drop lands where shown. → apps/android/app/src/main/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/EditorEngineBlocks.kt `planLift`, TextFirstRenderHold.kt `dragging`, apps/android/app/src/androidTest/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/TextFirstBlockDragEdgeTest.kt
- *(Android)* A lift starts no editing: no caret, no keyboard, through the drag and after the drop; the platform's pressed state, long press and the note's other touch handlers are handed a cancel at the lift, and the finger no longer scrolls the note (the edge autoscroll does). After a move the selection stays with its text and is not revealed. → apps/android/app/src/main/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/BlockLift.kt `begin`, TextFirstEditTextUpdates.kt
- *(Android)* The card is the block drawn from the note's own layout (from the part that shows at the lift), measured again whenever the layout changes (a photo decoding, a new width), 1.04 times its size after a 120 ms ease from 0.97, with 14 dp corners, the page colour at 80% behind opaque text, a 1 dp border and three shadows; at most 40% of the screen tall, its last 56 dp fading out when the block is taller. It floats over the whole window, keeps the offset from the finger it had at the lift and follows the finger. Until the gap first moves the block's place is dimmed to 35% in the note and scrolls with it. A photo lifted out of its line is its card and its dimmed place. → apps/android/app/src/main/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/BlockDrag.kt
- *(Android)* At the lift the view asks the engine, in one call, for the slots in the text on screen and a screen's height either side; as the note scrolls it asks only for the stretch newly in that range, keeping slots by their place in the text, never by where they were drawn at the lift.
- *(Android)* A lifted block's note reflows as a preview; nothing is edited until the drop. The gap starts at the block's own place (nothing would move) and moves past the content between two neighbouring slots when the finger's height passes that content's middle as painted now (a sliding neighbour where it shows), one way per move, so it never flicks back; a stretch with no slot (a quote, code, a table) is passed whole. A photo lifted out of its line stays home until the finger leaves that line. → apps/android/app/src/main/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/BlockReflow.kt `ReflowStops`, apps/android/app/src/test/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/BlockReflowTest.kt
- *(Android)* Once the gap has moved, the block's place closes, its copy dimmed to 35% sits in the gap, and the content between slides there over 180 ms with the web editor's easing; content scrolling in is drawn in place; no drop line. Each part is the note's own drawing moved, so content off screen at the lift is drawn too. A photo lifted out of its line leaves the line laid out without it; its copy is the photo as its own plain line. → apps/android/app/src/main/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/BlockReflowPreview.kt, BlockReflowTiles.kt, BlockReflowPhoto.kt, apps/android/app/src/androidTest/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/TextFirstBlockReflowTest.kt
- *(Android)* At the release the card goes at once, the copy turns solid and the move is made under the preview; once the moved note arrives every part on screen starts where the preview drew it and slides to where it is laid out within 180 ms. 400 ms after the release the preview ends, even if the editor has not made the move yet; until the move is made or refused the note takes no input (taps, boxes, links, holds), as the order it shows is about to change. The view does not scroll. Released at home or onto a refused move, what slid goes back. → apps/android/app/src/main/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/BlockReflowLanding.kt, BlockLift.kt `release`, `landed`
- *(Android)* With animations off in system settings, or no preview (logged under `BlockReflow` with why), the drag shows a drop line: 3 dp in the accent colour over the card, at the slot nearest the finger among those on screen, none while one of the block's own edges (the part that shows) is nearer, and for a lifted photo none while the finger is nearer its middle; nothing slides. → apps/android/app/src/main/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/BlockDragTargets.kt `SlotBook`, `DropTargets`, apps/android/app/src/test/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/BlockDragTargetsTest.kt
- *(Android)* With the drop line, releasing over it moves the block there as one Undo step, if the line shows on screen and the lifted block is laid out (a new width may cut a long view short of it); otherwise nothing moves. Releasing with no drop line, or onto a move the engine refuses, changes nothing and the card slides back to where its block is drawn now, the note scrolled or not. The note does not jump at the drop: the scroll stays, or, when the block left from above the screen, the line drawn just above the drop line stays where it was. → apps/android/app/src/main/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/BlockLift.kt `release`, BlockDrag.kt `holdFor`, apps/android/app/src/androidTest/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/TextFirstBlockDragTest.kt
- *(Android)* Haptics, through the view's haptic feedback (so the system's touch-feedback setting applies): a long-press impact at the lift, a tick (a segment tick from Android 14, a clock tick before) each time the finger brings the gap (or the drop line) to a slot it was not on, none while it stays, none over the block itself and none when the note scrolls under a still finger, and a gesture-end impact (a key click before Android 11) when the block lands. A release that moves nothing is silent. → apps/android/app/src/main/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/BlockLift.kt `DragHaptics`, apps/android/app/src/androidTest/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/TextFirstBlockDragTest.kt
- *(Android)* Held within 64 dp of the top or bottom edge of the note, a lifted block scrolls it: 200 dp/s at the zone's inner edge ramping to 1400 dp/s at the edge and beyond, every frame while the finger holds still, a frame's step at most 0.05 s of travel, stopping at the note's ends as the preview draws them (where it asks for no frames until there is room to scroll again) and whenever the drag ends; where the note shows less than two zones the nearer edge wins. The gap (or the drop line) follows the content under the still finger whenever the note scrolls, whatever scrolled it (a scroll restored after a resize too), without a tick, among the slots that scroll into view too, and the drop lands where it shows. → apps/android/app/src/main/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/BlockDragTargets.kt `BlockAutoscroll`, BlockLift.kt, apps/android/app/src/test/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/BlockDragTargetsTest.kt, apps/android/app/src/androidTest/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/TextFirstBlockDragEdgeTest.kt
- *(Android)* A change of layout alone during a drag (a photo finishing loading, a new width) measures the preview (or the dim and drop line) and the card again from the text as drawn now, and the drag carries on; a new width that cuts a long view short of the block sends the gap home, so a release moves nothing. A change to the note (its engine revision moving: an edit, a sync, an outside write, another block's move, a reload) cancels it at once with the finger still down: the card and the preview (or the drop line and the dim) go, nothing moves, no haptic, and nothing the finger does until it lifts is a drag. → apps/android/app/src/main/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/TextFirstEditTextUpdates.kt, BlockLift.kt `revisionSeen`, apps/android/app/src/androidTest/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/TextFirstBlockDragEdgeTest.kt
- *(Android)* A drag also ends with nothing moved, before anything reaches the editor, when the body or another field of the screen takes focus during it (a hardware key, accessibility focus), and when the system cancels the touch or withdraws the lifting finger (a palm, `FLAG_CANCELED`), when the window loses focus (a call, a system dialog, the app leaving the foreground) and when the note view detaches or its editor closes; a card still sliding home or landing goes with the view. A second finger during a drag is ignored; the drag follows the finger that lifted. → apps/android/app/src/main/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/BlockLift.kt `interrupt`, TextFirstEditText.kt `onWindowFocusChanged`, apps/android/app/src/androidTest/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/TextFirstBlockDragTest.kt, TextFirstBlockDragEdgeTest.kt
- *(Android)* Puppet's editor state reports the drag (`view.drag`): reflow or drop line, the gap's slot and line (null at home), and the dimmed copy's place in window px (landing: the moved block's; a photo back home: its box). → apps/android/app/src/debug/java/com/futo/notes/puppet/PuppetNativeEditorState.kt
