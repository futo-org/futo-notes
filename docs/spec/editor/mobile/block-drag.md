# Block drag

Moving a block by hand in the native editor (#311). The engine decides what a press lifts, where
it may land and the move ([portable-core block movement](../portable-core/block-movement.md));
the lines here are the gesture that names the position under the finger and the slot it lands on.

A long-press lifts a block only while the keyboard is down; while the note is being edited every
long-press is the platform's (#441). The keyboard counts as up whenever a text field of the note's
screen holds focus, so a hardware keyboard typing into the note counts too. The web editor asked
only whether its body had focus; here the title and Find fields count as well.

> **Gap:** Android has no block drag gesture yet (#444): a long-press there is still the platform's.

- *(iOS)* With the keyboard down, holding a finger 340 ms within 10 pt anywhere on a line of the note body (its margins included) lifts what the engine says a press at the position under the finger lifts: on a photo, that photo's position; elsewhere, the character nearest the finger on that row, whole: right of a row's last character the press is on that character's start, never inside it (an emoji's two UTF-16 units). → apps/ios/Sources/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstEditorController+BlockDrag.swift `TextFirstLiftPolicy`, apps/ios/Tests/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstBlockDragTests.swift, apps/ios/UITests/TextFirstBlockDragUITests.swift
- *(iOS)* While the keyboard is up (the body, an open table cell, the title or Find holds focus, the keyboard on screen or a hardware one) no press lifts and the long-press is the platform's as on a stock text view: the loupe, long-press selection, holding the caret to drag it, a selection's handles, dragging selected text, and a long-press on an image or a link. → apps/ios/Sources/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstEditorController+BlockDrag.swift `mayLift`, `armLift`, apps/ios/Tests/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstBlockDragTests.swift, apps/ios/UITests/TextFirstBlockDragUITests.swift
- *(iOS)* With the keyboard down, a long-press that lifts nothing keeps the platform's (the loupe, the text menu with Paste): on a blank line, the note's empty last line, front matter, code nothing closes, and anywhere in an empty note. → apps/ios/Sources/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstEditorController+BlockDrag.swift `liftPlan`, apps/ios/Tests/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstBlockDragTests.swift
- *(iOS)* While a long note is still opening no press lifts: the long-press is the platform's everywhere in the note. The title field is not part of the body and keeps its own. → apps/ios/Sources/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstEditorController+BlockDrag.swift `mayLift`
- *(iOS)* With the keyboard down, a long-press on a photo that shares its Markdown line with other content lifts the photo alone and opens nothing: in "Nice!![](photo.jpg)" the photo lifts without "Nice!", and dropped just below (or above) its line it becomes a line of its own. → apps/ios/Sources/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstEditorController+BlockDrag.swift `liftPlan`, apps/ios/Tests/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstBlockDragTests.swift
- *(iOS)* A long-press on that line's text, or beside the photo on its row (off the photo), lifts the whole line, photo included. → apps/ios/Sources/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstEditorController+BlockDrag.swift `pressOffset`, apps/ios/Tests/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstBlockDragTests.swift
- *(iOS)* A long-press on a photo alone on its line, or in a quote or a table, lifts what its line lifts (on a table's grid, the whole table); one on a link lifts its line. Neither opens anything. → apps/ios/Tests/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstBlockDragTests.swift
- *(iOS)* With the keyboard down, the platform's long-presses wait for the lift and act only where it lifts nothing, and its text drag is off; a press that lifts starts neither the loupe nor long-press selection. Whether a press may lift is settled as its first finger touches down, so with the keyboard up nothing waits on the lift. A double-tap still selects a word, and a tap, a checkbox tap, vertical scrolling and a table's sideways scroll are unchanged. → apps/ios/Sources/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstEditorController+BlockDrag.swift `armLift`, apps/ios/Sources/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstEditorController+Taps.swift, apps/ios/UITests/TextFirstBlockDragUITests.swift
- *(iOS)* A lift starts no editing: no caret, and the keyboard stays down through the drag and after the drop. At the lift an open edit menu closes and the note stops scrolling until the finger lifts; after a move the selection stays with its text. → apps/ios/Sources/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstEditorController+BlockDrag.swift `beginBlockDrag`, apps/ios/UITests/TextFirstBlockDragUITests.swift
- *(iOS)* The lifted block is a card, a snapshot of the block at its full on-screen size (the part that shows, for a block running off screen) with a see-through background, that follows the finger; the block's place in the note is dimmed. A lifted photo's card, and the dimmed place, are the photo alone. Neighbouring blocks do not move. → apps/ios/Sources/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstEditorController+BlockDrag.swift `TextFirstBlockDrag`
- *(iOS)* A drop line marks the engine's slot whose boundary is nearest the finger, among the slots that show; when one of the block's own two edges is nearer there is no drop line. A lifted photo has no such edges (every slot moves it, its own line's edges too): there is no drop line while the finger is nearer the photo's middle than any slot. → apps/ios/Sources/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstEditorController+BlockDrag.swift `TextFirstDropTargets`, apps/ios/Tests/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstBlockDragTests.swift
- *(iOS)* Releasing over a drop line moves the block there as one Undo step. Releasing with no drop line, onto a move the engine refuses, or after the note changed during the drag, changes nothing and the card slides back to its place. → apps/ios/Sources/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstEditorController+BlockDrag.swift `endBlockDrag`, apps/ios/UITests/TextFirstBlockDragUITests.swift
- *(iOS)* Haptics, as the web editor's: a medium impact at the lift, a selection tick each time the drop line reaches a slot it was not on (none while it stays, none for hiding over the block itself), and a light impact when the block lands. A release that moves nothing returns the card silently. → apps/ios/Sources/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstEditorController+BlockDrag.swift `TextFirstDragHaptics`, `TextFirstBlockDrag.follow`, apps/ios/Tests/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstBlockDragTests.swift
