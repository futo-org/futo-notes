# Block drag

Moving a block by hand in the native editor (#311). The engine decides what a press lifts, where
it may land and the move ([portable-core block movement](../portable-core/block-movement.md));
the lines here are the gesture that names the position under the finger and the slot it lands on.

A long-press lifts a block only while the keyboard is down; while the note is being edited every
long-press is the platform's (#441). The keyboard counts as up whenever a text field of the note's
screen holds focus, so a hardware keyboard typing into the note counts too. The web editor asked
only whether its body had focus; here the title and Find fields count as well.

- *(iOS)* With the keyboard down, holding a finger 340 ms within 10 pt anywhere on a line of the note body (its margins included) lifts what the engine says a press at the position under the finger lifts: on a photo, that photo's position; elsewhere, the character nearest the finger on that row, whole: right of a row's last character the press is on that character's start, never inside it (an emoji's two UTF-16 units). → apps/ios/Sources/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstEditorController+BlockDrag.swift `TextFirstLiftPolicy`, apps/ios/Tests/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstBlockDragTests.swift, apps/ios/UITests/TextFirstBlockDragUITests.swift
- *(iOS)* While the keyboard is up (the body, an open table cell, the title or Find holds focus, the keyboard on screen or a hardware one) no press lifts and the long-press is the platform's as on a stock text view: the loupe, long-press selection, holding the caret to drag it, a selection's handles, dragging selected text, and a long-press on an image or a link. → apps/ios/Sources/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstEditorController+BlockDrag.swift `mayLift`, `armLift`, apps/ios/Tests/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstBlockDragTests.swift, apps/ios/UITests/TextFirstBlockDragUITests.swift
- *(iOS)* With the keyboard down, a long-press that lifts nothing keeps the platform's (the loupe, the text menu with Paste): on a blank line, the note's empty last line after a final line ending, front matter, code nothing closes, and anywhere in an empty note. → apps/ios/Sources/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstEditorController+BlockLift.swift `liftPlan`, apps/ios/Tests/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstBlockDragTests.swift
- *(iOS)* An empty task on the note's last line, which draws its box and no characters, lifts like any other line: its card and its dimmed place are its row. Dropped above it a block lands above the task, dropped below its row after it: two drop lines, at the top and the bottom of the row. The row is the one its box is drawn on, also where TextKit lays it out inside the row above (`- [ ] milk\n- [ ] \n- [ ] `): a press, the card and both drop lines use it, and a press on the row above lifts that row. → apps/ios/Sources/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstLayoutFragments.swift `trailingRowFrame`, apps/ios/Tests/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstEmptyLastRowTests.swift → apps/ios/Sources/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstEditorController+BlockLift.swift `boundaryY`, apps/ios/Tests/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstBlockDragTests.swift, apps/ios/UITests/TextFirstBlockDragUITests.swift
- *(iOS)* While a long note is still opening no press lifts: the long-press is the platform's everywhere in the note. The title field is not part of the body and keeps its own. → apps/ios/Sources/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstEditorController+BlockDrag.swift `mayLift`
- *(iOS)* With the keyboard down, a long-press on a photo that shares its Markdown line with other content lifts the photo alone and opens nothing: in "Nice!![](photo.jpg)" the photo lifts without "Nice!", and dropped just below (or above) its line it becomes a line of its own. → apps/ios/Sources/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstEditorController+BlockLift.swift `liftPlan`, apps/ios/Tests/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstBlockDragTests.swift
- *(iOS)* A long-press on that line's text, or beside the photo on its row (off the photo), lifts the whole line, photo included. → apps/ios/Sources/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstEditorController+BlockLift.swift `pressOffset`, apps/ios/Tests/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstBlockDragTests.swift
- *(iOS)* A long-press on a photo alone on its line, or in a quote or a table, lifts what its line lifts (on a table's grid, the whole table); one on a link lifts its line. Neither opens anything. → apps/ios/Tests/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstBlockDragTests.swift
- *(iOS)* With the keyboard down, the platform's long-presses wait for the lift and act only where it lifts nothing, and its text drag is off; a press that lifts starts neither the loupe nor long-press selection. Whether a press may lift is settled as its first finger touches down, so with the keyboard up nothing waits on the lift. A double-tap still selects a word, and a tap, a checkbox tap, vertical scrolling and a table's sideways scroll are unchanged. → apps/ios/Sources/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstEditorController+BlockDrag.swift `armLift`, apps/ios/Sources/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstEditorController+Taps.swift, apps/ios/UITests/TextFirstBlockDragUITests.swift
- *(iOS)* A lift starts no editing: no caret, and the keyboard stays down through the drag and after the drop. At the lift an open edit menu closes and the note stops scrolling until the finger lifts; after a move the selection stays with its text. → apps/ios/Sources/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstEditorController+BlockDrag.swift `beginBlockDrag`, apps/ios/UITests/TextFirstBlockDragUITests.swift
- *(iOS)* The lifted block is a card, a snapshot of the block at its full on-screen size (the part that shows, for a block running off screen) with a see-through background, that follows the finger; the block's place in the note is dimmed. A lifted photo's card, and the dimmed place, are the photo alone. Neighbouring blocks do not move. → apps/ios/Sources/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstEditorController+BlockDrag.swift `TextFirstBlockDrag`
- *(iOS)* At the lift the view asks the engine, in one call, only for the slots inside what TextKit has laid out (the screen and a margin), so a lift is as quick in a long note as in a short one. Every slot the engine offers is one where the drop will be accepted, so a drop line never promises a move that springs back. → apps/ios/Sources/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstEditorController+BlockLift.swift `liftPlan`, apps/ios/Sources/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstEditorController+BlockMove.swift `blockSlots`
- *(iOS)* A drop line marks the engine's slot whose boundary is nearest the finger, among the slots that show; when one of the block's own two edges is nearer there is no drop line. A lifted photo has no such edges (every slot moves it, its own line's edges too): there is no drop line while the finger is nearer the photo's middle than any slot. A photo alone on its line lifts its line, and the card, the dimmed area and those two edges are the line's, even on the note's last line where the photo's range and its line's are the same; the view tells the two apart by whether what the engine returned runs from a line's start to a line's start. → apps/ios/Sources/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstEditorController+BlockDrag.swift `TextFirstDropTargets`, apps/ios/Tests/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstBlockDragTests.swift
- *(iOS)* Releasing over a drop line moves the block there as one Undo step. Releasing with no drop line, onto a move the engine refuses, or after the note changed during the drag, changes nothing and the card slides back to its place. → apps/ios/Sources/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstEditorController+BlockDrag.swift `endBlockDrag`, apps/ios/UITests/TextFirstBlockDragUITests.swift
- *(iOS)* A block dropped into a list arrives as the engine writes it ([block arrival](../portable-core/block-arrival.md)): a plain line or a photo dropped into a checklist becomes an unchecked task, a bullet dropped into a numbered list takes its number and the items below count on, a nested item dropped among top-level items comes out to their level, and an item dropped outside any list goes to the left margin. The card and the dimmed place show the block as it looked where it was lifted; the changes appear at the drop, in the same Undo step. → crates/futo-notes-editor/src/engine/commands/lists/arrival.rs, crates/futo-notes-editor/src/engine/tests/moves/arrival.rs
- *(iOS)* Haptics, as the web editor's: a medium impact at the lift, a selection tick each time the drop line reaches a slot it was not on (none while it stays, none for hiding over the block itself), and a light impact when the block lands. A release that moves nothing returns the card silently. → apps/ios/Sources/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstEditorController+BlockDrag.swift `TextFirstDragHaptics`, `TextFirstBlockDrag.follow`, apps/ios/Tests/Editor/TextFirst/TextFirstBlockDragTests.swift

## Android

The Android editor (#444) follows the lines above where they say what a lift, a drop line and a
drop do; it differs only where a line below says so. Its lift, card and drop line are
apps/android/app/src/main/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/BlockLift.kt, BlockDrag.kt and
BlockDragGeometry.kt; its engine calls EditorEngineBlocks.kt.

- *(Android)* With no text field of the note's screen focused (the body, an open table cell, the title or Find), holding a finger 340 ms within 10 dp anywhere on a line of the note body lifts what the engine says a press at the position under the finger lifts: on a photo, that photo's position; elsewhere the character nearest the finger on that row, whole (right of a row's last character, that character's start; a position inside a photo the finger is beside steps past it). A finger that starts scrolling the note first (past the platform's touch slop, 8 dp) lifts nothing. → apps/android/app/src/main/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/BlockLift.kt `LiftPolicy`, BlockDragGeometry.kt `pressOffset`, apps/android/app/src/androidTest/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/TextFirstBlockDragTest.kt
- *(Android)* While a field is focused, a hardware keyboard's included, no press lifts and the long-press is the platform's: word selection with its handles and text menu. → apps/android/app/src/main/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/BlockLift.kt `mayLift`, TextFirstController.kt `liftAllowed`, apps/android/app/src/androidTest/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/TextFirstBlockDragTest.kt
- *(Android)* The press asks the engine at 340 ms; the platform's long press (at the system's long-press timeout) waits for the answer and runs only where nothing lifts: a blank line, the empty last line after a final line ending, front matter, code nothing closes, an empty note. A press that lifts gets neither the platform's selection nor its text menu; taps, double- and triple-taps, a checkbox tap, scrolling and a table's sideways scroll are unchanged. A table's grid, a link and a checkbox are held like any other part of their line. → apps/android/app/src/main/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/BlockLift.kt `holdsLongPress`, TextFirstEditTextTouch.kt, apps/android/app/src/androidTest/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/TextFirstBlockDragTest.kt
- *(Android)* While the note is still opening (before its editor session exists) no press lifts. Once it is open, a lift in a note whose end is still streaming into the view first loads the rest of it, then lifts, and the drop lands where the drop line showed. → apps/android/app/src/main/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/EditorEngineBlocks.kt `planLift`, apps/android/app/src/androidTest/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/TextFirstBlockDragTest.kt
- *(Android)* A lift starts no editing: no caret, no keyboard, through the drag and after the drop; the platform's pressed state, long press and the note's other touch handlers are handed a cancel at the lift, and the finger no longer scrolls the note (the edge autoscroll does). After a move the selection stays with its text and is not revealed. → apps/android/app/src/main/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/BlockLift.kt `begin`, TextFirstEditTextUpdates.kt
- *(Android)* The card is the block drawn from the note's own layout (the part that shows at the lift), 1.04 times its size after a 120 ms ease from 0.97, with 14 dp corners, the page colour at 80% behind opaque text, a 1 dp border and three shadows; at most 40% of the screen tall, its last 56 dp fading out when the block is taller. It floats over the whole window, keeps the offset from the finger it had at the lift and follows the finger. The block's place is dimmed to 35% in the note and scrolls with it. A photo lifted out of its line is its card and its dimmed place. → apps/android/app/src/main/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/BlockDrag.kt
- *(Android)* At the lift the view asks the engine, in one call, for the slots in the text on screen and a screen's height either side; as the note scrolls it asks only for the stretch newly in that range, keeping slots by their place in the text, never by where they were drawn at the lift. The drop line, 3 dp in the accent colour over the card, marks the slot nearest the finger among those on screen, none while one of the block's own edges (the part that shows) is nearer, and for a lifted photo none while the finger is nearer its middle. → apps/android/app/src/main/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/BlockDragTargets.kt `SlotBook`, `DropTargets`, apps/android/app/src/test/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/BlockDragTargetsTest.kt
- *(Android)* Releasing over a drop line moves the block there as one Undo step. Releasing with no drop line, or onto a move the engine refuses, changes nothing and the card slides back to its place. The note does not jump at the drop: the scroll stays, or, when the block left from above the screen, the line drawn just above the drop line stays where it was. → apps/android/app/src/main/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/BlockLift.kt `release`, BlockDrag.kt `holdFor`, apps/android/app/src/androidTest/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/TextFirstBlockDragTest.kt
- *(Android)* Haptics, through the view's haptic feedback (so the system's touch-feedback setting applies): a long-press impact at the lift, a tick (a segment tick from Android 14, a clock tick before) each time the finger brings the drop line to a slot it was not on, none while it stays, none over the block itself and none when the note scrolls under a still finger, and a gesture-end impact (a key click before Android 11) when the block lands. A release that moves nothing is silent. → apps/android/app/src/main/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/BlockLift.kt `DragHaptics`, apps/android/app/src/androidTest/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/TextFirstBlockDragTest.kt
- *(Android)* Held within 64 dp of the top or bottom edge of the note, a lifted block scrolls it: 200 dp/s at the zone's inner edge ramping to 1400 dp/s at the edge and beyond, every frame while the finger holds still, a frame's step at most 0.05 s of travel, stopping at the note's ends and whenever the drag ends; where the note shows less than two zones the nearer edge wins. The drop line follows the slot now under the still finger, among the slots that scroll into view too, and the drop lands where it shows. → apps/android/app/src/main/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/BlockDragTargets.kt `BlockAutoscroll`, BlockLift.kt, apps/android/app/src/test/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/BlockDragTargetsTest.kt, apps/android/app/src/androidTest/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/TextFirstBlockDragTest.kt
- *(Android)* A change of layout alone during a drag (a photo finishing loading, a new width) measures the dim, the card and the drop line again from where the text is drawn now, and the drag carries on. A change to the note's text (a sync, an outside write) cancels it at once with the finger still down: the card, the drop line and the dim go, nothing moves, no haptic, and nothing the finger does until it lifts is a drag. → apps/android/app/src/main/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/TextFirstEditTextUpdates.kt, BlockLift.kt `noteChanged`, apps/android/app/src/androidTest/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/TextFirstBlockDragTest.kt
- *(Android)* A drag also ends with nothing moved when the system cancels the touch, when the window loses focus (a call, a system dialog, the app leaving the foreground) and when the note view detaches or its editor closes. A second finger during a drag is ignored; the drag follows the finger that lifted. → apps/android/app/src/main/java/com/futo/notes/textfirst/BlockLift.kt `interrupt`, TextFirstEditText.kt `onWindowFocusChanged`
