# Inline formatting

The rebuilt core (the `engine` feature, #423) does what this file states; the shipping editor
still runs the old engine until the cut-over. The cases are
`crates/futo-notes-editor/tests/fixtures/engine/inline.json`.

- Inline marks open and close on one line ([linedown.md](linedown.md)): bold, italic, strikethrough and inline code show without their marks, and marks a change does not touch keep their spelling. Code shows its text literally. → crates/futo-notes-editor/src/lines/inline/mod.rs, crates/futo-notes-editor/src/engine/line.rs
- Enter and Shift+Enter on an unquoted plain line split the bold, italic, strikethrough, code and link marks the caret is inside, as on headings, quotes and list items: they close before the break and open again after it, with the caret after them (`**bo|ld**` becomes `**bo**` and `**ld**`; a link becomes two links with the same destination). At a mark's text start the break goes before the whole mark, at its text end after it. Anywhere else Enter writes its line break as typed. Backspace joining the two halves again leaves two marks side by side (`[ab](u)[cd](u)`). → crates/futo-notes-editor/src/engine/commands/blocks/keys.rs `plain_enter`, crates/futo-notes-editor/src/engine/commands/marks.rs `split`
- A format toggle at a collapsed caret writes nothing: it turns the format on or off in the formats typing at that caret carries (the toolbar shows them), and those formats end when the caret moves or a command runs. Typing there writes the marks around what is typed so it reads in exactly those formats and every other character keeps its own (`**ab‸cd**` with Bold off, typing `x`, gives `**ab**x**cd**`); this is the one place typing writes marks. A space typed at a bold, italic or strike span's end lands outside its marks, whether or not the toolbar set formats and whether or not the host's input range is the selection; the caret then carries the span's formats, so the next character joins the span again in the span's own spelling (`**hello w**`, `__a b__`). Where the line cannot spell the formats there, the text lands as plain typing does. A toggle on a code line or in front matter does nothing. → crates/futo-notes-editor/src/engine/commands/inline/typing.rs, crates/futo-notes-editor/src/engine/commands/inline/mod.rs `toggle_format_view`
- A toggle over a selection turns the format on for every selected visible character unless all of them already carry it, then off for all of them, keeping the visible text and the selection. A toggle writes the house spelling: `**`, `*`, `~~` and a backtick run longer than any inside the text (padded by a space when the text starts or ends with a backtick); `__` and `_` are used where an asterisk cannot open or close. → crates/futo-notes-editor/src/engine/commands/inline/format/mod.rs `respell`
- Marks go where they read: spaces at a selection's ends stay outside them, a span the selection reaches or touches becomes part of one span with it, part of a span taken off splits it (`**a⟦b⟧c**` gives `**a**b**c**`), a format crossing an edge closes and reopens around the new marks, and part of a code span, link address, wikilink, image or tag takes the format whole. The line is read back before anything is kept; nothing is escaped. → crates/futo-notes-editor/src/engine/commands/inline/format.rs, crates/futo-notes-editor/src/engine/commands/inline/draft.rs
- A toggle over several lines formats each line's text on its own, and each table cell's, including cells past the header (#419); it skips blank lines, dividers, code and front matter lines and table delimiter rows. A `|` line that is not in a table is a plain line, formatted whole. No mark crosses a line or cell, and no line changes its block. → crates/futo-notes-editor/src/engine/commands/inline/mod.rs `format_command`
- A toggle over a selection is one host acceptance and one Undo step restoring exact bytes and selection. A line the format cannot be spelled on is left as it is; when no line can take it the toggle refuses with `UnrepresentableFormat` and changes nothing. Its work is linear in the selected lines and no more than the old engine's on the same note. → crates/futo-notes-editor/src/engine/commands/inline/mod.rs, crates/futo-notes-editor/src/engine/tests/inline.rs
- A text-service replacement that ends at a collapsed caret with carried formats (a completed or autocorrected word, a keyboard's double-space period) is typing: a word the caret's own formats covered, or one the service changed rather than extended, takes the carried formats whole, a word inside a link's text included (`[a hel](u)` completed under Bold reads `[a **hello**](u)`), and the marks of a span the word filled go with it, so a completion ending in a space reads as typed (`**hel**` with Bold turned off, completed to `hello `, gives `hello `; with Bold still on, `**hello** `); a word of mixed formats or holding a whole link only on the letters the service added (`*he*l` completed to `hello` under Bold reads `*he*l**lo**`). The carried formats stay for what is typed next. → crates/futo-notes-editor/src/engine/commands/inline/typing.rs `formatted_input`
- Replacing a selection keeps the marks of spans reaching out of it and drops the marks of a span it empties; the typed text lands inside spans that start where the selection does. A deletion that leaves a bold, italic or strike span with a space at its edge (`**b c**` less `c`) moves the space outside the marks (`**b** `), so the span keeps its format, except at a line's start, where a leading space would indent the line. → crates/futo-notes-editor/src/engine/edit/plan.rs, crates/futo-notes-editor/src/engine/commands/inline/typing.rs `kept_formats`
- Backspace and Delete at a collapsed caret keep the formats it carries, so deleting a space typed after italic text and typing on continues the italic.
- A deletion the caret no longer reads the deleted text's formats after (it emptied their span, as clearing `**Owner**` from a table cell does, or moved a space outside the marks) leaves the caret carrying the formats of the first deleted character, as Cut does, so the next character typed takes them, as in a rich-text editor. This holds for Backspace, Delete, a replacement with nothing, a text service's and a composition's; text replacing a selection is typing instead. The formats end when the caret moves or a command runs; Undo and Redo restore them with the selection. Over a selection the toolbar's and `active_formats` formats are those every selected character carries. → crates/futo-notes-editor/src/engine/commands/inline/typing.rs `deleted_formats`, `carry_deleted` → crates/futo-notes-editor/src/engine/commands/inline/typing.rs
