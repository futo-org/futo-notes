# List Indent and Outdent

- A line's indent is its own leading whitespace after its quote markers: a space is one column, a tab four (linedown.md). There is no depth: Indent and Outdent change the leading whitespace of the lines they move, and numbers (below), and nothing else. → crates/futo-notes-editor/src/engine/commands/lists/indent.rs
- Indent writes one tab after the item's quote markers, before any whitespace it already has, for bullets, numbers and tasks alike, empty items and a list's first item included (`- a\n- b` becomes `- a\n\t- b`, `> - a\n> - b` becomes `> - a\n> \t- b`). Every item the selection touches moves. → crates/futo-notes-editor/src/engine/commands/lists/indent.rs
- Outdent gives the item the leading whitespace of the item it sits under: the nearest list item above it at the same quote depth with less indent, found past blank lines, lines indented further and fences under an item. The item then lines up with that one whatever mix of spaces and tabs the note uses (`- a\n  - b\n    - c` with Outdent on `c` gives `- a\n  - b\n  - c`; `\t\t- c` under `\t- b` gives `\t- c`). With no such item it takes off one tab, or every space when there is no tab. An item with no indent is not outdented: nothing changes and no Undo step is made. → crates/futo-notes-editor/src/engine/commands/lists/indent.rs `space_above`
- The lines under an item move with it: the lines after it at its quote depth that are indented further (items, indented plain lines, and a fence opened under it with all its code), down to the first line that is not. Indent puts one tab after their quote markers too; Outdent replaces the item's old whitespace at their start with its new whitespace. Code lines whose whitespace does not start with the item's keep theirs, and empty code lines gain no tab. A selected item under another selected item moves once, with it. → crates/futo-notes-editor/src/engine/commands/lists/indent.rs `under`
- Numbered items renumber; only items with the same delimiter and quote depth count, and each walk stops at the first item out of sequence (lists.md). Indent makes the item count on from the numbered item above it at its new indent, or start a new sub-list at 1, and the items it left that were counting on after it close up (`1. a\n2. b\n3. c\n4. d` with Indent on `c` gives `1. a\n2. b\n\t1. c\n3. d`). Outdent makes it count on from the numbered item above it at its new indent (with none it keeps its number), bumps the items after it at that indent that were counting on, and the items it left that were counting on after it now sit under it and count again from 1 (`1. a\n   1. b\n   2. c\n2. d` with Outdent on `b` gives `1. a\n2. b\n   1. c\n3. d`). → crates/futo-notes-editor/src/engine/commands/lists/indent.rs, crates/futo-notes-editor/src/engine/commands/lists/number.rs
- Enter on an empty indented item is Outdent (an empty item's own way out of a nesting level); Enter on an empty item with no indent takes it out of the list (lists.md). → crates/futo-notes-editor/src/engine/commands/lists/enter.rs
- Tab and Shift+Tab with the caret or selection touching a list item run Indent and Outdent, with the same source, selection and one Undo action as the buttons. On lines with no list item Tab is not an indent key: it changes nothing and inserts no tab character (code keys are fenced-code.md's). → crates/futo-notes-editor/src/engine/commands/lists/mod.rs `list_key`
- `ToolbarState.can_indent` is true when the caret's line is a list item; `can_outdent` when that item has leading whitespace. Plain lines, headings, quote lines (a quote changes level only through the Quote button), tables and code are not moved by these buttons, and the engine reports both false there. → crates/futo-notes-editor/src/engine/commands/lists/mod.rs `list_indents`, crates/futo-notes-editor/src/engine/surface.rs `toolbar`
- An item whose text is a quote marker (`- > q`) indents and outdents like any other; no item is refused. → crates/futo-notes-editor/tests/fixtures/engine/lists/indent.json
