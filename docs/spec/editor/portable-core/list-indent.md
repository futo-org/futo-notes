# List Indent and Outdent

- Tab and Shift+Tab with the caret or selection in a list item (outside code) run the Indent and Outdent commands on the supplied range, as the desktop editor's Tab nests and un-nests an item (interactive-elements.md): the same source, selection and one Undo action as the toolbar buttons. Where that button is disabled (`ToolbarState.can_indent`/`can_outdent`, see the Indent line below), the key changes nothing and inserts no tab character; in a plain paragraph, heading or quote Tab is not an indent key. → crates/futo-notes-editor/src/session/blocks/commands.rs `list_tab`, crates/futo-notes-editor/tests/lists/tab.rs
- Indent and Outdent on a list item nest and un-nest it, and a list item is never refused for its shape (decided 2026-11-09). Indent nests the item, with its sub-items, under the item above it; Outdent moves it out one level. Indent nests with a tab, as Obsidian does (decided 2026-11-09): one `\t` per level after any `>` quote markers on the line, never before them, so a quoted list stays in its quote (`- a\n- b` becomes `- a\n\t- b`, `> - a\n> - b` becomes `> - a\n> \t- b`), for bullets, numbers and tasks alike; the item's sub-items and the lines of its body move one level with it. Where a number's wider marker leaves a single tab short of the parent's text (an ordered item in a quote), the tab stop after it is used (`> 1. a\n> 2. b` becomes `> 1. a\n> \t\t1. b`). An item that joins an existing sub-list is written at that list's own marker column, so a space-indented sub-list in an older note keeps its spaces. Outdent removes one level: one leading tab, or, on a line an older note indented with spaces, the spaces of one nesting level (`- a\n  - b\n    - c` with Outdent on `c` gives `- a\n  - b\n  - c`); a line that was tab-indented stays tab-indented. Ordered lists renumber: the new sub-list counts from 1 and the siblings that remain close up (Indent on `c` in `1. a\n2. b\n3. c\n4. d` gives `1. a\n2. b\n\t1. c\n3. d`), and the same on the way out: an outdented numbered item rejoins its parent list with the next number and keeps its own sub-items and its former following siblings nested under it, as one list counting on from the sub-items. This holds at every depth, for any mix of bullets, numbers and tasks (a numbered item under a bullet, a bullet or task under a numbered item), and inside quotes. Nothing is repaired, respelled or closed, and no list item shape is turned away. If the list edit cannot respell a shape (`- a\n- > q`), the item still moves by one tab after the quote markers, or one tab stop of whitespace comes off. The button is greyed out only where nothing sensible can happen: Indent on the first item of a list (nothing above it to nest under; any indent there would turn it into a code block or lazy text, and the desktop editor does nothing there too), and Outdent on a top-level item or on an item that shares its line with its parent (`- - a`), which has no indentation to take off. Enter and Backspace on an empty item, not the Outdent button, take it out of its list. An Indent or Outdent that would change nothing makes no Undo step; any other is one Undo action that restores the exact bytes and selection. Paragraphs, headings, plain quote lines (a quote changes level only through the Quote button) and tables are not indented: the buttons stay visible but greyed out, and the engine reports `can_indent` and `can_outdent` false. → crates/futo-notes-editor/src/session/blocks/indent_command.rs, crates/futo-notes-editor/src/session/blocks/list_edit/indent.rs, crates/futo-notes-editor/src/session/toolbar.rs, crates/futo-notes-editor/tests/lists/command_eligibility.rs
- Indent on an empty item (a new item after Enter) nests it under its previous sibling like any other item, with the caret staying on it (decided 2026-11-06, as in Apple Notes and Google Docs; written without a blank line 2026-11-09, as in Obsidian): `- a\n- ` becomes `- a\n\t- `, `1. a\n2. ` becomes `1. a\n\t1. ` (a new ordered level counts from 1), a task item `- [ ] a\n- [ ] ` becomes `- [ ] a\n\t- [ ] `, and an item whose sibling already has a sub-list joins it (`- a\n\t- b\n- ` becomes `- a\n\t- b\n\t- `). The new line is read as an empty nested item, never as an underline that turns the parent into a heading (see lists.md), so Indent writes only the tab and the marker, and typing after it lands in the nested item (`- a\n\t- b`). Outdent of that nested item returns it one level out. Tab and Shift+Tab and `ToolbarState.can_indent`/`can_outdent` follow the same eligibility; each is one Undo action restoring the exact source and selection. → crates/futo-notes-editor/src/session/blocks/list_edit/indent.rs, crates/futo-notes-editor/src/session/toolbar.rs, vendor/pulldown-cmark/src/firstpass.rs, crates/futo-notes-editor/tests/lists/commands.rs, crates/futo-notes-editor/tests/lists/indent_empty_item.rs, crates/futo-notes-editor/tests/lists/nesting_examples.rs, crates/futo-notes-editor/tests/lists/command_eligibility.rs, crates/futo-notes-editor/tests/inline/toolbar_refusals.rs
