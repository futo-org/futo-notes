# Persistence, recovery and outside writers

- The store accepts each completed edit into an unbuffered recovery stream before returning its display change; process exit can lose the operation being applied, but reopening replays every previously accepted edit. OS acceptance alone is not a power-loss durability acknowledgement. → crates/futo-notes-store/src/editor_session/recovery.rs
- Recovery records stay in the vault's hidden `.futo-editor-recovery` directory, excluded from visible note listings and sync inventory; the versioned header binds the stream to its canonical vault root, note id, exact base and base revision. SHA-256 frames protect the header and records against accidental corruption. The first edit initializes the stream with its exact base. → crates/futo-notes-store/src/editor_session/recovery.rs
- One process can attach only one session to a note's recovery stream, including through different store handles. Independent app processes editing the same vault remain unsupported, as for the existing local note engine. → crates/futo-notes-store/src/editor_session/lease.rs
- Detaching or switching leaves accepted edits recoverable without an implicit markdown checkpoint; an intentionally emptied attached note remains a present empty note, while opening an absent note without recovery does not create it. → crates/futo-notes-store/tests/editor_lifecycle.rs
- Atomic rename, move and folder workflows retarget live and detached recovery to the complete mutation's collision-resolved identity; failed preparation is canceled before later input can be accepted. A committed move whose recovery cannot finish retains its discoverable intent and pauses input until retry. → crates/futo-notes-store/src/editor_session/identity.rs, crates/futo-notes-store/src/editor_session/recovery/identity.rs
- A successful own-action backlink rewrite adopts source only when the live accepted source still equals its pre-action disk source and no composition is active; otherwise it preserves the exact draft, selection and history against the rewritten saved baseline. Detached backlink drafts keep outside-writer protection. → crates/futo-notes-store/src/editor_session/identity.rs
- Actual adopted relinks map existing Undo and Redo through the canonical text-level wikilink rule without adding an Undo step; identity-only, failed and skipped rewrites preserve history verbatim, including historical-only links. Selection follows unchanged scalars and uses affinity at replaced interiors. → crates/futo-notes-editor/src/session/relink.rs
- Relink adoption prepares the same final canonical source for every scoped historical field before acceptance; a URI becoming literal preserves retained Code and literal pre-Code history, while an actual incomplete typed seed gaining complete URI meaning keeps activation with subsequent input. Refusal leaves source, selection, transient lifetimes and both stacks unchanged. → crates/futo-notes-editor/tests/relink/affected_literal.rs, crates/futo-notes-editor/tests/relink/typed_class.rs, crates/futo-notes-store/src/tests/editor_session/relink_guarded/literal.rs
- The baseline-aware draft rename/move workflows follow a matching attachment to its authoritative final identity, including a parked draft at the requested destination; a stale peer source and its backlinks remain untouched. → crates/futo-notes-store/src/editor_draft.rs, crates/futo-notes-store/src/editor_session/checkpoint.rs
- A committed local note delete terminates that attachment, including a pending incoming identity transition; late input, checkpoints, resume and cached portable requests cannot revive it. Its durable decision retires only captured recovery artifacts, leaving an intentionally recreated filename as a new note. Failed deletion restores its protected draft, and incomplete rollback cleanup pauses input until repaired. Folder deletion retains its existing move-notes-up policy. → crates/futo-notes-store/src/editor_session/deletion.rs, crates/futo-notes-store/src/folders.rs
- Recovery header v3 can retain exact accepted source separately from its saved baseline at an identity boundary; framed v2 streams remain readable, while incompatible versions remain retained and paused. Persistent Undo across reopening is not included. → crates/futo-notes-store/src/editor_session/recovery_format.rs
- Checkpoint owns the write/converge/recreate/park decision and log retirement under the store gate and vault mutation guard. It durably records the chosen destination before installation and returns the existing flush disposition and complete mutation; the installed note is synced before the log is retired. → crates/futo-notes-store/src/editor_session/checkpoint.rs, crates/futo-notes-store/src/flush.rs
- A checkpoint that commits markdown but fails its durability check or recovery retirement reports the committed disposition and complete mutation in `CheckpointCommitted`; the draft remains copyable, edits pause, and checkpoint can retry the committed save decision after revalidating its destination. A markdown-write failure leaves the healthy recovery stream available and allows further protected edits. → crates/futo-notes-store/src/editor_session/checkpoint.rs
- Recovery status exposes monotonic accepted and durable revision watermarks within an attached session, including Undo/Redo and checkpoint; append acceptance advances only accepted, while a successful flush or durable checkpoint advances durable. Recovery durability does not mean markdown has been checkpointed. Reopening conservatively treats replayed records as accepted until another successful flush acknowledges them. → crates/futo-notes-store/src/editor_session/mod.rs, crates/futo-notes-store/src/editor_session/checkpoint.rs
- The first unflushed edit sets a provisional 100 ms deadline in an injected monotonic clock; further edits do not postpone it. Hosts can query the remaining delay without knowing the clock origin and run due flushes on a background worker; timer execution alone never acknowledges storage. Successful flushes report deadline lateness measured at completion, including storage time. This core scheduling contract is not a device durability-window measurement. → crates/futo-notes-store/src/editor_session/clock.rs, crates/futo-notes-store/src/editor_session/recovery.rs
- Append, short-write and flush failures report their stage, retain the current accepted draft for copying, and pause Replace/Undo/Redo without advancing either watermark for rejected edits. Resume repairs a failed live append's unaccepted tail or an explicitly reported torn final record and must successfully flush the accepted prefix before unpausing; failed repair remains paused. → crates/futo-notes-store/src/editor_session/recovery.rs
- Recovery tests exercise real public editor/store workflows above an injected filesystem: process death retains OS-accepted bytes, while modeled power loss discards unflushed bytes and preserves successful flushes; a failed flush never advances the durable prefix. → crates/futo-notes-store/src/tests/editor_session/durability.rs, crates/futo-notes-store/src/tests/editor_session/storage.rs
- A torn final record opens the validated draft prefix paused, reports its record number and retains the original log bytes; explicit resume truncates only that incomplete tail and successfully flushes the prefix before accepting further edits. → crates/futo-notes-store/src/tests/editor_session/recovery
- Corrupt complete records, invalid edit footprints, duplicate/out-of-order revisions, wrong note/vault identity and incompatible formats report a typed recovery issue, retain the log and keep editing/checkpoint paused; no later records are skipped or guessed into place. The validated prefix remains copyable, while an untrusted header opens the current disk source for copying. → crates/futo-notes-store/src/editor_session/recovery_format.rs
- A valid stream whose base differs from the current note retains its exact recovered draft and reports the mismatch; checkpoint preserves both versions through the existing persist-or-park workflow. → crates/futo-notes-store/src/tests/editor_session/recovery
- Shared vault destination installers durably record local replacement intent before controlled writes, creates, note moves, rollback restores, bootstrap backup recovery, text migration and sync adoption, including absent destinations whose older recovery stream has the same base bytes; recovery parks the validated draft instead of undoing the installed version. Only the attached editor's own checkpoint id is exempt; a foreign park destination remains protected. → crates/futo-notes-core/src/files/vault_fs.rs, crates/futo-notes-store/tests/destination_recovery.rs
- Once a vault has a recovery directory, directory installs retain a hidden destination-prefix counter before rename, protecting every delivered descendant, including external source entries created after the workflow's mapping snapshot. File and prefix replacement counters share the existing collision identity across case and Unicode aliases while filenames retain their exact spelling. Recovery compares the checked sum of retained literal leaf, shared leaf and all ancestor counters without changing the persisted header schema; corrupt or exhausted counters fail closed. → crates/futo-notes-core/src/files/recovery_coordination.rs, crates/futo-notes-store/tests/destination_recovery
- Bootstrap holds the vault mutation guard across parallel backup recovery and text migration; coordination failure retains the backup and sidecar and reports a warning for retry. Metadata writes bypass visible-note replacement policy without suppressing it for other installers. → crates/futo-notes-store/src/bootstrap.rs, crates/futo-notes-core/src/files/parked_backup.rs
- Recovery append and stream repair share the vault mutation guard with controlled writes, so the first accepted edit cannot initialize its log inside an outside writer's decision/install window; a vault without a recovery directory needs no replacement metadata until that first append. Native and portable hosts use the same acceptance boundary. → crates/futo-notes-store/src/editor_session/recovery.rs, crates/futo-notes-store/src/tests/editor_session/recovery/outside_writes.rs
- A committed park whose destination already owns another pending recovery stream reports `CheckpointCommitted`, retains the original stream and keeps input/resume paused before retargeting; recovering the destination's draft first lets checkpoint retry finish without deleting or appending to foreign recovery bytes. → crates/futo-notes-store/src/editor_session/checkpoint.rs, crates/futo-notes-store/src/tests/editor_session/recovery/outside_writes.rs
- A checkpoint retry revalidates its previously committed destination under the vault guard and refreshes its complete positioned mutation before retiring recovery; if another writer replaced that destination, the draft returns through persist-or-park. → crates/futo-notes-store/src/editor_session/checkpoint.rs
- Reopening an interrupted checkpoint recognizes its exact installed draft at the recorded destination and resumes retirement with a complete mutation, including a parked target from an earlier calendar day; repeated recovery creates no additional conflict copy. Process-death checks interrupt before installation, before retirement and after retirement. → crates/futo-notes-store/src/tests/editor_session/recovery
