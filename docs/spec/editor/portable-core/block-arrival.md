# Block arrival

What changes when a moved block ([block movement](block-movement.md)) arrives in a list or leaves one (#442). A block arriving strictly between two lines of a list joins that list; elsewhere it keeps its marker. → crates/futo-notes-editor/src/engine/commands/lists/arrival.rs, crates/futo-notes-editor/src/engine/tests/moves/arrival.rs

- Strictly between two lines of a list the first line of the block becomes a sibling of the item below the slot and takes its leading whitespace as spelled (tabs included); the lines under it move by the same whitespace, as Indent moves them, and keep their markers (`- a\n  - b\n  - c` with `x` dropped above `c` gives `- a\n  - b\n  - x\n  - c`; a nested `  - b` dropped above a top-level item becomes `- b`). → crates/futo-notes-editor/src/engine/commands/lists/shift.rs
- It adopts the marker style of the item below: its bullet character (`* a\n* b` with `- x` dropped between gives `* x`), or numbering with its delimiter, and the space after the marker. A plain line, an indented plain line lifted alone, or a photo lifted out of its line becomes an item (`- [ ] a\n- [ ] b` with `plain` dropped between gives `- [ ] plain`; `Nice!![](a.jpg)` with the photo dropped there gives `- [ ] ![](a.jpg)`). Where its text would then read as more on its own (a box), one `\` is written before it, as the list buttons do (`1. [ ] n` arriving among bullets gives `- \[ ] n`).
- When the item below the slot is a task it gains an unchecked box. A box it already has is kept with its state (`* [x] done` arriving among `- ` items gives `- [x] done`); a numbered item cannot hold a box, so among numbered items a task keeps its own bullet and box, and the numbers around it stay.
- When the line below the slot is a line under an item rather than an item, the block takes that line's whitespace and keeps its own marker, a plain line taking `- ` (`- a\n  note` with `x` dropped between gives `- a\n  - x\n  note`), so the line below still moves with the item above it when that item is indented or lifted.
- Numbered lists renumber at both ends of the move under the rule editing follows (lists.md), and which items each end rewrites is decided from the note as it was before the move. Where a numbered item left, its siblings after it that were counting on from it (same level, same delimiter, in sequence) each count one less, wherever the block lands (`1. a\n2. b\n  - child\n3. c` with `a` dropped above `child` gives `1. b\n  - a\n  - child\n2. c`). Where a block arrives as a numbered item it counts on from the numbered sibling above the slot, as that sibling ends up, or repeats its number when the item below has it too (a list numbered alike); with no numbered sibling above it takes the number of the item below, or 1 when that number is too long to count with. When it counts up, the item below and its siblings after it that were counting on from it each count one more, unless the item below and the next one are numbered alike (`1. a\n2. b\n2. c\n3. d` with `c` dropped above `b` gives `2. c\n2. b\n2. d`). A walk, and the look for that next one, passes over lines indented further and over the moved lines while they were in the sequence, and stops where the note before the move stopped it, never reaching a list the move joined to it (`1. a\n2. b\ncut\n3. d\n3. e` with `cut` dropped above `b` gives `2. cut\n3. b` and leaves `3. d\n3. e`). A line on both walks keeps its number, and a number written with leading zeros keeps its width. In `1. a\n2. b\n3. c\n4. d`, `a` dropped above `d` gives `1. b\n2. c\n3. a\n4. d`. → crates/futo-notes-editor/src/engine/commands/lists/arrival/numbers.rs
- A hand-numbered list that was not counting in sequence is left alone: `1. a\n5. b\n9. c` with `x` dropped above `5. b` gives `2. x` and nothing else renumbers.
- A list item dropped outside any list (directly above a list's first line or below its last included) goes to the left margin with its marker, number and box as written, the lines under it moving with it (`  - b\n    - c` gives `- b\n  - c`); `3. c` dropped above `1. a\n2. b` stays `3. c`. The list it left still closes up.
- An indented plain line dropped outside any list loses its indent, with a `\` where its text would otherwise read as another block on its own (`  # not` gives `\# not`); one that only the lines around it would make more (`  |---|` dropped under a pipe line, which would make it a table header) is refused, as the guard refuses any such move. A plain line dropped directly above a list's first item or directly below its last stays a plain line, and a photo dropped outside a list is a plain line of its own.
- A block that is no unquoted list item or plain line (a quote run, whatever its first line's indent, a code block, a table, a heading, a divider) lands as written, byte for byte: `>   - a\n> b` dropped at the end of `>   - a\n> b\nz` keeps its markers. → crates/futo-notes-editor/src/engine/tests/moves/trial.rs
- Whether a slot is offered reads the first line as it arrives (with the digits of the item below standing in for its number) and the lines under it shifted, one at a time as the reading reaches them; the renumbered lines elsewhere are not read, since a number never changes how a line reads. The lines the move may renumber (the arriving line when it takes a number, and the numbered items at the level the moved item left and at the level it lands on) are compared without their digits and every other line as written, so a move that would change only the numbers it rewrites is no move (`1. a\n2. a\n3. b` with the first `a` dropped above `b`), while `- A\n  4. Apples\n- A\n  8. Apples\n- B` trades its subtrees. Whether a drop changes anything is read from at most 64 lines, the moved lines where they land first: a drop past a longer run of lines that read like the lines they replace is no move. → crates/futo-notes-editor/src/engine/commands/moves/order.rs, crates/futo-notes-editor/src/engine/command/trial.rs `trial_changes`, crates/futo-notes-editor/src/engine/command/trial/changes.rs
