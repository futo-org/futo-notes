# v1.9.1

A follow-up to 1.9.0: everyone can now pick a second app icon, and we fixed several bugs in syncing, naming notes and the editor.

What's new

- Every install can now choose the Reversed app icon (white mole on orange) in Settings, no license needed. A license still unlocks the other icons.

Fixes

- Sync now stops trusting a certificate authority as soon as you remove it from your device. Before, a sync connection that was already open could keep working until you restarted the app.
- Naming a note the same as another note (even with different capitalization, like "Test" and "test") now warns you and keeps your text.
- On iPhone running iOS 26, the find bar no longer opens hidden behind the keyboard.
- Under-the-hood fixes and cleanup.

## Short

Everyone can now choose the Reversed app icon. Sync stops trusting a certificate authority as soon as you remove it. Duplicate note names are caught regardless of capitalization and no longer lose your text.
