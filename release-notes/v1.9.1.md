# v1.9.1

A follow-up to 1.9.0: everyone can now pick a second app icon, and we fixed several bugs in syncing, naming notes and the editor.

What's new

- Every install can now choose the Reversed app icon (white mole on orange) in settings on iPhone, iPad and Android, no license needed. A license still unlocks the other icons.

Fixes

- Sync now stops trusting a certificate authority as soon as you remove it from your device. Before, a sync connection that was already open could keep working until you restarted the app.
- Naming a note the same as another note (even with different capitalization, like "Test" and "test") now warns you and keeps your text. On desktop, a new note with a duplicate title no longer loses what you typed when you navigate away.
- On iPhone running iOS 26, the find bar no longer opens hidden behind the keyboard.
- On desktop, dragging a block to the top or bottom edge of the window now scrolls the note.
- Under-the-hood fixes and cleanup.

## Short

Everyone can now choose the Reversed app icon. Sync stops trusting a certificate authority as soon as you remove it. Duplicate note names are caught regardless of capitalization and no longer lose your text. Fixes the iOS 26 find bar hiding behind the keyboard.
