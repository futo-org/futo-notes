# v1.9.3

A small bug-fix release.

Fixes

- Opening Settings on iPad no longer crashes the app.
- On the desktop app, a vault folder that has been moved or disconnected no longer files a crash report from the home screen. The app asks you to reconfigure it instead.
- Notes whose file names contain characters such as a colon or question mark, often made by other apps, were hidden from the list. They are now renamed without those characters when the app starts, so they show up and sync like any other note.

## Short

Notes with a colon or question mark in the file name, often made by other apps, are renamed so they show up and sync again.
