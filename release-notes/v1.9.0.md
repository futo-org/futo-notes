# v1.9.0

This is one of our biggest releases yet. We have a new editor, the ability to purchase a license, custom app icons on mobile, and much more.

New Editor

The new editor now feels much more like a rich text editor, while maintaining the same promise that FUTO Notes set out with: all your notes are markdown files.

While you will still be able to use markdown syntax to style your text, you'll notice that markdown symbols are now hidden in most scenarios. You maintain the power of markdown with the feel of rich text.

You can now re-order blocks by dragging and dropping. / commands will show you all the elements you can add. Tables are much improved. You now have undo and redo options on mobile!

We've put a lot of effort into the editor and would love your feedback. You can leave your feedback in settings.

Pay us!

FUTO's philosophy has always been to make great software that users want to pay for. We hope that we've earned your money by this point. Now you can purchase a license to use the app - you'll get nice cosmetic bonuses when you do.

A license bought in the App Store works in FUTO Notes on iPhone and iPad with your Apple Account. It doesn't come with a license key, so it can't be used on other platforms.

Many other new features

- sort your notes by name or last modified, ascending or descending
- license holders can switch out the app icon in settings (mobile only)
- the app now trusts your device's CA certificates

Performance and bugs

- the app opens faster, as do larger notes
- sharing a very large note no longer crashes the app
- many small bug fixes

## Short

One of our biggest releases yet. The new editor feels like rich text while your notes stay markdown files: drag to re-order blocks, use / commands, enjoy better tables, and undo and redo on mobile. You can now purchase a license to use the app through FUTOpay at pay.futo.org, and license holders can choose the app icon on mobile. Sort notes by name or last modified. The app opens faster, and sharing a very large note on Android no longer crashes the app.
