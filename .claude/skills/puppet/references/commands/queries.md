`{"json":true}` on any query, or in a `do` step's args, gives the raw body instead of the text.

**Finding things.** A note's text is read where it shows, as a person reads it: `doc`,
`show {"text"}` and receipts see the lines on screen, while `state.editor` names the note
it holds (`note`) and which lines show (`onScreen`). Text off screen is found as a person finds
it: Find in the note (`OpenFind`, `SetFindQuery`, `StepFind`) brings the match on screen,
and `ScrollToLine {"line"}` scrolls to a line in one call. On the page, `elements | grep`
finds a row.

**Notes.** A note's id is its vault-relative path without `.md` — `grocery list`,
`work/standup` — and the filename _is_ the title (AGENTS.md M2). Every note verb
takes that id. `OpenNote {"noteId"}` runs what clicking the note's row runs (`"mode":
"foreground"` or `"background"` is the modifier click that opens it in a new tab), so
use it on a row that is on screen: expand the folders on its path (`ToggleFolder`)
and bring the row into view (`ScrollToRow`, or the real `scroll`) until `elements`
lists it, as a person would. `OpenNote` and the other verbs that act on one row (`OpenFolder`,
`ToggleFolder`, the row menus, a row's move and delete) refuse with 409 when the row is not
on screen, printing the batch that brings it there and runs the verb (`do '[…]'`, the
`GoBack`s a phone needs included); an id the vault does not have is the app's to answer. `notes` is the vault's
own answer to "does this exist, and where is it now", and the one to trust after
a move, rename or delete: the sidebar draws nothing inside a collapsed folder,
and deleting a folder's last note deletes the folder with it. The rows that _are_
drawn carry their ids — `note=<id>`, `folder=<path>`.
On the phones the lists are native: `elements` lists their rows with the same `note=` and
`folder=`, and `notes` answers from the store itself. Saves are debounced, so the file appears
a moment after the verb; the file on disk is the oracle for what landed.
