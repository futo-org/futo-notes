# `just puppet` — the command surface

The skill (`.claude/skills/puppet/SKILL.md`) owns how to use these well. This file
is the reference: what exists, what it takes, and what it answers.

- [Queries](commands/queries.md): `look`, `actions`, `state`, `notes`, `search`, `file`, `doc`, `elements`, `show`, `at`, `events`, `diff`, `clipboard`, `screenshot`, `relaunch` and `step`: what each answers and takes; finding things; note ids and their rows.
- [Gestures, scrolling and keys](commands/gestures-and-keys.md): real `click`/`tap`/`drag`/`scroll`, `type` and `press` on desktop and the phones, and the `ScrollBy`/`ScrollToRow` shortcuts.
- [Editor verbs](commands/editor-verbs.md): the rendered document and its positions, fast writes against typed input, and every editor verb with its arguments.
- [Where the other verbs live](commands/verb-map.md): the verbs each screen offers on desktop, iOS and Android, sync and license, confirmations.
- [Receipts, refusals, exit codes](commands/receipts.md): what a receipt prints, its JSON form, the status codes and exit codes.
